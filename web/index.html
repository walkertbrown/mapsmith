Mapsmith

Maps for your table from a seed: a walled town, the country around it, a dungeon, a spaceship, or the inside of a building. Click a number to see what's there and who keeps it. The bar over the map changes it, and the buttons under it save it.

The place's note

Mapsmith Pro, $6 once on itch: Save for virtual tabletop writes a .dd2vtt with every wall, door, window and light placed. Roll20 imports it as it is; Foundry reads it with the Universal Battlemap Importer module. One key works here and in the Obsidian plugin.

Keep your campaign in Obsidian? Mapsmith is a free plugin there too, and the map lives in your note: Settings, Community plugins, Browse, search Mapsmith. See it in Obsidian's list.
