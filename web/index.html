A fantasy town map generator

	
Pick a size from hamlet to city, water (a river, a coast, a river mouth or a lake), flat land, hills or mountains, and walls or none. Every map has its market, temple, inn, smithy, harbour and more numbered, and each one has a name, a keeper, a line about it and a hook. Every building opens into a floor plan of its inside.

	
The country around it

	
A region map is about 40 km across: towns and villages along the river or the shore, roads that go round the hills, woods, and the odd ruined keep, barrow or old mine. Each place opens into its own town or dungeon map.

	
Dungeon map generator

	
Crypts, fallen keeps and old mines, from a four-room lair to a deep level of sixteen rooms with stairs further down. Rooms are joined by doors, arches, barred gates and secret doors, furnished, and stocked with hazards and treasure.

	
Spaceship deck plans

	
Shuttles, corvettes, freighters and stations, intact with a crew or derelict with a story of what went wrong, on steel plate and grating.

	
Maps for Foundry, Roll20 and Owlbear Rodeo

	
Save as picture gives the players' map with no numbers and secret doors as plain wall, named with its size in squares so Owlbear Rodeo sets the grid. With Mapsmith Pro, Save for virtual tabletop writes a Universal VTT file (.dd2vtt) with the walls, doors, windows and lights already placed, so line of sight works the moment it's imported. How a town comes into Foundry and Roll20.

	
Play it solo

	
Explore hides the map: walk a town one street at a time, or open a dungeon or a derelict room by room, with what you meet rolled as you go. Search a room for secret doors on a 5 or 6.

	
Share a map

	
The page's address always holds the map you're looking at. Copy link to this map and send it to your players or your group, and it opens on the same map.




Your map and key stay in this browser. The page goes online only to check a Pro key or mail one. Mapsmith is open source (MIT). Made by Bananafest Destiny. The code was written with AI, and the textures under the maps came from an image generator; the maps are drawn by code.
